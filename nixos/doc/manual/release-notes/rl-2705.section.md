## New Modules {#sec-release-27.05-new-modules}

<!-- To avoid merge conflicts, consider adding your item at an arbitrary place in the list instead. -->

- [AIOMetadata](https://github.com/cedya77/aiometadata), a Stremio addon that provides metadata and catalogs from many sources. Available as [services.aiometadata](#opt-services.aiometadata.enable).

- [AIOStreams](https://aiostreams.org), a Stremio addon that consolidates multiple addons and debrid services into one. Available as [services.aiostreams](#opt-services.aiostreams.enable).

- [StremThru](https://docs.stremthru.13377001.xyz), a companion for Stremio that proxies debrid services and provides Stremio addons. Available as [services.stremthru](#opt-services.stremthru.enable).

## Backward Incompatibilities {#sec-release-27.05-incompatibilities}

<!-- To avoid merge conflicts, consider adding your item at an arbitrary place in the list instead. -->

- [](#opt-services.mealie.settings) has a proper type now:
  - Booleans are coerced to `"true"` and `"false"` instead of `"1"` and `""`.
  - Values other than booleans, ints, strings, paths or packages will be rejected.
  - `null` values now unset the value instead of producing an empty string.
